    \section{Overview}
        This paper considers an online resource optimization problem for robot computation systems. Informally, given a robot system equipped with multiple computation tasks to run, we want to perform online scheduling optimization to achieve good overall (computational) safety and performance as the robot moves into different, unknown, environments.

        To that end, we first build theoretical models to quantify the safety and performance metrics for a robot computation system, then we propose efficient online optimization algorithms to optimize priority assignments and tasks' run-time configurations under different environments.

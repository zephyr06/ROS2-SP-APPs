
	\section{Conclusion}
    This paper presents novel scheduling and optimization algorithms to enhance the safety and performance of robot systems in dynamic environments. Robot applications often face varying spatial and temporal computation demands, necessitating adaptive system-level resource allocation to maintain safety and performance. Achieving such optimization online is challenging due to the problem's complexity and resource constraints.

    To address this, we introduced novel safety-performance metric functions to evaluate system performance and developed efficient optimization algorithms. These algorithms leverage the classical priority assignment approach and incremental optimization to exploit the continuity of system changes effectively. We evaluated the proposed scheduling algorithm on a robot system, with experimental results demonstrating 20\% to 40\% improvements in overall performance under dynamic conditions.
